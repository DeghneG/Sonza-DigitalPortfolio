# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Professors and a school panel reviewing graded coursework (Prelim, Midterm, Finals). They need to find each term's project fast and judge it from a clear case study.

## Product Purpose
Personal digital portfolio of John Khyne E. Sonza, a Computer Science student at the University of San Agustin. Success is a panel member understanding who he is, what he can build, and reading the One ID, One Connect case study without friction.

## Positioning
A student portfolio organised by academic term (Prelim / Midterm / Finals) with real case studies, not a generic developer landing page.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Stack: Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind + GSAP; all copy in src/content/site.ts; useReveal hook must stay in use. Routes: `/`, `/contact`, `/work/one-id-one-connect`, `/resume.pdf` (file not yet supplied). Sections per sitemap: About (bio, Python/Java/C/PHP/JavaScript/SQL), Credentials (Colegio De San Jose 2018-2022, St. Roberts International School 2022-2024, University of San Agustin 2024-Now), Contact (Jksonza@usa.edu.ph), Work. Case study page: problem/overview; role/timeline/tools; process/wireframes/code/visuals; results/deliverables; previous/next links. Footer: GitHub, Email.

## Brand Commitments
Mainly black with white; masculine; premium and minimalist; seamless. The sitemap's terminal-window motif (windows with dots, `$ /path` prompts, mono labels) is the user's own. Avoid: neon/hacker-green cliche; template "big heading + cards" layout.

## Evidence on Hand
Only the sitemap PDF and placeholder copy. No case-study content, screenshots, GitHub URL or resume file yet; none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles
1. The work is findable by term in one move.
2. Identity is carried by the name, set as a statement.
3. Restraint reads as confidence.
